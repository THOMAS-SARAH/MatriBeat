MatriBeats AI Architecture & Signal Pipeline

How raw single/multi-lead abdominal biopotentials are filtered, unmixed using FastICA, and analyzed for dual-heart risk classification.

1

Abdominal Input

Single composite signal contains high-power maternal ECG + attenuated fetal ECG + muscle noise.

2

DSP Preprocessing

SciPy 0.5-45Hz Butterworth Bandpass filter removes baseline drift and 50/60Hz powerline interference.

3

ICA Separation

FastICA (Blind Source Separation) decomposes independent source components to isolate mECG from fECG.

4

Peak & HRV Extraction

Pan-Tompkins adaptive peak detection identifies R-peaks and computes instantaneous BPM & HRV.

5

Risk Classifier

Evaluates fetal baseline, variability, and maternal stress to generate real-time clinical alerts.
